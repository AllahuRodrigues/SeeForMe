# External datasets: what is on this machine, what needs your account

Status as of 2026-10-08. Everything lives under `data/` (gitignored) and is regenerable with the scripts named below.
`data/manifests/datasets.json` and `data/manifests/models.json` are the machine-readable registry with licences and commercial-use flags (`python scripts/build_manifests.py`); `research/source_registry.csv` is the research bundle's source list; `scripts/dataset_inventory.py` lists what is present on disk.

| dataset | role in SeeForMe | access | local state | how to get it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EgoBlind** (NeurIPS 2025 D&B) | real blind-user egocentric video; weak scheduling events from the users' questions; replay training and evaluation | public Google Drive, research only | `external/EgoBlind` (code), all 673 train videos, test videos pending (Drive throttles the test zips), caches and scenarios built | `make egoblind-download` keeps retrying the throttled zips every 20 min; see `docs/egoblind.md` |
| **GuideDog** (ACL 2026) | depth-reliability calibration (383 closer/farther pairs) and hazard coverage of the 2,106 human-verified gold guidance paragraphs (hazards by clock direction) | Hugging Face, gated, auto-approved, CC BY-NC 4.0 plus gate terms (academic research only, no redistribution of the data or derived data) | gold, depth and object downloaded (`data/guidedog`, 0.65 GB); silver (4.5 GB) not yet | `.venv/bin/hf auth login` once, then `make guidedog`; silver: `python scripts/download_guidedog.py --configs silver` |
| **DADA-2000** | out-of-distribution accident video (dashcam viewpoint; never pedestrian ground truth) | Google Drive / Baidu | only the annotation sheet (`data/dada/dada_annotations.xlsx`, 123 clips categorised) | the video archive is a 117 GB split zip (DADA2000.zip + .z01-.z05, all six parts required to extract) and does not fit next to EgoBlind on this disk; an external drive is needed. The simulated `ood_aftermath` scenes stand in for it |
| **BLV-Road-Nav-Accessibility** (Islam et al., ASSETS 2024) | the 90 "crucial objects" taxonomy from blind and low-vision navigation, 671 labelled keyframes with presence labels: measures what the detector vocabulary covers | GitHub, public (no videos in the repo) | `external/BLV-Road-Nav-Accessibility` (256 MB); `results/blvnav/taxonomy_map.md`, `detector_eval.md` | `python scripts/blvnav_taxonomy.py` (`--skip-detector` for the map only). Finding: YOLO11n names 20 / 90 objects, 2 / 13 of the high-priority groups; the open-vocabulary prompts raise it to 49 / 90 |
| **Mendeley navigation-assistance dataset** (DOI 10.17632/66bjx8h79p.1, CC BY 4.0) | 35,261 frames of pre-extracted detector features (22 Dhaka street classes, distances, angles, motion) with a Move/Stop decision label | Mendeley Data, public | `data/mendeley_navassist/` (87 MB); `results/mendeley_navassist/summary.md` | `python scripts/mendeley_navassist.py`. Finding: the label is a locomotion command recoverable from their own features (0.90 CV accuracy, majority 0.71), no timing, no images: a weak "something action-relevant ahead" target at best |
| **BlindNavigationMultimodalDataset** (Google Drive folder `1unHMorAjR4jWkzqaDxxCXe2okDfET-Np`, "Time-Aware Assistive Navigation", under review 2025, CC BY 4.0) | image + clip + navigation prompt + instruction + `speaking_reason` + planned action | Google Drive | not downloaded: `videos.tar.gz` 537 GB, `real_test.tar.gz` 38 GB, `images.tar.gz` 32 GB; only the croissant metadata was read | the three label JSONs (~415 MB: `train_labels.json`, `test_labels.json`, `real_test.json`) carry the "reason for speaking" field and are worth fetching on their own if the question is *why* an assistant spoke |
| **TIMELI** (ICRA 2026, "Time-Aware Assistive Navigation"; the Drive folder above is its data) | per-second *when-to-speak* labels: instruction or silence with a `speaking_reason` (obstacle in front, junction, direction change, constant instruction, remain silent), 269 CARLA videos + 11 real clips | Google Drive, public, CC BY 4.0 | `data/timeli/` (471 MB: the three label JSONs, the generator and closed-loop scorer code, `events.jsonl` with 24,937 SeeForMe events); frames and videos (32-537 GB) not downloaded | `python scripts/timeli_inspect.py`, `python scripts/timeli_to_events.py`; `docs/timeli_notes.md`. The closest external dataset to SeeForMe's question, but its silence means "no rule fired" (not "user confirmed clear") and the generator speaks 12-17 times a minute |
| **VizWiz-Classification** (CVPR 2023) | 8,900 photos by blind users with quality flags (blur, dark, bright, framing, obstruction, rotation): detector robustness by capture flaw | public, CC BY 4.0, no login | `data/vizwiz_cls/` (699 MB: annotations + a 1,500-photo stratified sample); `results/vizwiz/summary.md` | `python scripts/vizwiz_robustness.py`; `docs/vizwiz_notes.md`. Finding: YOLO11n's recall of labelled objects falls 0.57 -> 0.45 -> 0.35 with 1 / 2 / 3+ flaws (rotation worst, 0.32) while its confidence only drops 0.58 -> 0.49, so a frame-quality gate is the better uncertainty input than detector confidence |
| **Mobilio** (Liu and Slade, Nature BME 2026) | reference for a shipped BLV navigation aid: obstacle beeps, spatial-audio heading cue, personalised audio parameters, 14-participant study logs | GitHub, MIT | `external/Mobilio` (172 MB: 4 C# algorithm scripts and the study data; the app and weights are not in the repo) | `docs/mobilio_notes.md` (algorithms with constants, data columns, the -41% contacts and -12% time reproduced from the logs) |
| **WalkVLM-LR** (arXiv 2508.16070, ICASSP 2026) | a VLM walking assistant with an environment-awareness discriminator that gates generation (danger grades A/B/C) and four human-preference rewards | code public (archived snapshot), weights not released; WAD / WRD data 129-375 GB without a licence | `external/walkvlm` (10 MB) | `docs/walkvlm_lr_notes.md` (rewards, discriminator, TRF metric, comparison with per-object event windows). Reusable idea: a class-agnostic danger grade for `unknown_obstacle` and the EgoBlind vocabulary gap |
| **ORBIT** (Massiceti et al.) | personal-object teachable recognition videos from 67 blind / low-vision collectors, per-frame boxes and seven quality-issue labels | figshare, CC BY 4.0 | not downloaded (smallest benchmark zip 4.15 GB); `docs/orbit_notes.md` | FIND-mode test bed and capture-quality calibration; nothing about when to speak |
| **JRDB** | crowds, 2D/3D tracks, social groups (robot vantage) | registration with Monash (sign-in) | not downloaded | register at https://jrdb.erc.monash.edu.au/register/, then download the image sequences you want; a loader is not written yet |
| **Ego4D** | large-scale egocentric diversity, negative scenes | license agreement, credentials by email (~48 h) | `ego4d` CLI installed in the venv; no data | sign at https://ego4d-data.org/docs/start-here/, then `ego4d --output_directory=data/ego4d --datasets annotations` and a small `--benchmarks` subset, not the 7 TB |
| **Aria Everyday Activities** | camera motion, metric geometry, pose | sign-up on projectaria.com, CDN links file (14-day expiry) | `projectaria-tools` has no wheel for Python 3.13 (needs a 3.12 venv); no data | sign up at https://www.projectaria.com/datasets/aea/, download the links JSON, `aria_dataset_downloader -c <json> -o data/aria -l loc1_script1_seq1_rec1` for one sequence |
| **TextOCR / HierText** | scene-text OCR training | public | not downloaded (OCR training is out of scope; EasyOCR is used as is) | https://textvqa.org/textocr/ (7 GB), https://github.com/google-research-datasets/hiertext |
| **Mapillary Vistas / traffic signs** | street segmentation, sign appearance | registration / API terms | not downloaded | not needed for the scheduler experiments |
| **Research bundle** (`research/`) | scenario catalog (154 templates, 22 domains), sign taxonomy (89 strings), stress matrix (1,232 specifications), blueprint | local | integrated; `cd research && python -m pytest test_catalog.py` | `python research/sample_coverage.py --per-case 8` regenerates the matrix |

What the catalog became in code (none of it is annotated real data; see `docs/research_v2.md`):

* crowd_contention -> CampusBench kind `contention` (external speech on the channel, two hazards with one deadline, critical message playing when a cyclist crosses);
* lost_observation / poor_capture -> kind `occlusion` (hazard hidden 2 to 10 s by an occluder or a deliberate camera pan; in 30% of seeds it is gone when the view clears) and the tracker memory ledger (`--memory-ttl`);
* airport_terminal / transit_station / multilingual_and_symbols / text_and_signs -> kind `wayfinding_airport` (translated signage from `seeforme/cues/sign_vocab.py`, one goal, luggage, queues) and the sign taxonomy study (`scripts/eval_sign_taxonomy.py`);
* accident_aftermath / extreme_rare -> kind `ood_aftermath` (evaluation only: debris, glass, a stopped car, smoke-like capture loss, unknown obstacles that must be hedged);
* the nine stress modifiers -> `scripts/run_stress_matrix.py` (light, camera, density, speech channel, perception, goal, reaction time, language applied to CampusBench scenes and EgoBlind replays).
