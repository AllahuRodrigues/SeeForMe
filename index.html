SeeForMe: Predictive, Action-Aware Cue Scheduling for Blind and Low-Vision Navigation

CPSC 4900 Senior Project, Spring 2027 · Alláh-u-Abhá Rodrigues · Advisor: to be confirmed (course instructor: Prof. Sohee Kim Park)

Abstract

Camera-based assistive apps can detect objects, read text and estimate depth in real time, but they are poor at deciding what to say and when. SeeForMe treats spoken feedback as a scarce, budgeted channel. A scheduling layer (P-CACS) sits between tracked perception events and speech: it uses metric depth, time-to-contact and path-intersection probability to choose between SILENCE, SPEAK, INTERRUPT and HAPTIC actions, and composes the minimum message a user needs for their next safe action. The scheduler is evaluated on CampusBench, a metric simulator whose ground truth is a communication window per event, and will be validated on real campus video against confidence- and proximity-based baselines.
